Free Online Image Converter

ImageToolLab's Image Converter lets you convert images between all popular formats instantly. Whether you need to convert WebP to PNG, JPG to WebP, PNG to AVIF, or any other combination, our tool handles it all.

Supported Formats

  • PNG: Best for images with transparency, logos, and screenshots
  • JPG/JPEG: Best for photographs, small file sizes
  • WebP: Modern format, smaller files than PNG/JPG with similar quality
  • AVIF: Next-gen format, even better compression than WebP
  • GIF: Animated images, limited to 256 colors
  • BMP: Uncompressed bitmap format

Why Convert Images?

  • WebP/AVIF — Smaller files mean faster website loading and better SEO rankings
  • PNG — When you need transparency or lossless quality
  • JPG — Maximum compatibility, smallest photo file sizes
